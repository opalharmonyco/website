About

Opal Harmony is a privately held software company based in Thessaloniki, Greece. It was founded in April 2024 and is registered in the Greek General Commercial Registry (G.E.MI.) under no. 176908106000.

We work on two things: software products of our own, and application development for clients who need a small, careful engineering team.

Services

Application design & development

From first sketch to a shipped product: scoping, design, implementation and maintenance.

Web & mobile applications

Browser and iOS/Android apps built on a solid, tested foundation.

Backend systems & integrations

APIs, data pipelines and the connections between the services you already run.

Products

Alongside client work we build and publish our own software applications for consumers and small businesses.
