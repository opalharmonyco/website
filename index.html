Independent software company · Thessaloniki, Greece · est. 2024

What we do

We design and build small, focused software products — tools that do one job well, keep your data on your own device, and don't track you.

The company is privately held, founded in April 2024 and registered in the Greek General Commercial Registry (G.E.MI.). We also take on contract software engineering and research for selected clients.

Products

Taxfold

A receipt and expense log for iPhone (Mac to follow). Receipts stay on your device; nothing is uploaded, no account, no analytics. Built for freelancers and small companies who need a clean record at tax time.
